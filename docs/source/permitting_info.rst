Additional Resources: Permitting Info
=====================================

Permitting Overview
-------------------

This section provides supplementary permitting information for tidal energy systems in Alaska.

Due to the lack of specific permit cost data, permitting costs are not included in the Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) calculation in the software tool. However, a comprehensive list of agencies requiring permits or permissions has been identified to assist users in navigating the permitting process.

Relevant Agencies
-----------------

The following agencies may have permitting requirements for permanent installations in Alaska:

- **U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (USACE)**:
  - Regulates structures, anchors, and seabed disturbances in navigable waters.
  - **Permit Required**: Anchor placement and seabed cable or weighted line.

- **U.S. Coast Guard**:
  - Oversees navigation safety, lighting/marking, and obstructions in navigable waterways.
  - **Permit Required**: Private Aid to Navigation (PATON) permit for floating platforms.

- **Alaska Department of Natural Resources (ADNR)**:
  - Manages state-owned submerged lands.
  - **Permit Required**: Land use authorization or easement for anchor and undersea cable.

- **Alaska Department of Fish and Game (ADF&G)**:
  - Protects habitats and wildlife.
  - **Consultation Required**: Wildlife impact testing and habitat protection.

- **NOAA Fisheries**:
  - Regulates marine mammal interactions.
  - **Consultation Required**: If marine mammal interaction is plausible.

- **Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC)**:
  - Regulates hydroelectric projects under the Federal Powers Act.
  - **Exempt**: Microgrid application, not grid-connected, and not dam-related.

- **Local Municipality/Borough**:
  - Controls shore access, upland cable landing, and zoning.
  - **Permit Required**: Site-specific permit if the cable lands on municipal property.

- **Private Landowner/Tribal Entity**:
  - Manages access to private or tribal lands.
  - **Permit Required**: Easement or license required to operate on private land.

- **Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)**:
  - Controls discharges, spills, and environmental compliance.
  - **Permit Required**: Only if technology has discharge or runoff (likely exempt).

- **Bureau of Ocean Energy Management (BOEM)**:
  - Oversees federal offshore energy leasing.
  - **Exempt**: Permit not required for near-shore tidal passes.
